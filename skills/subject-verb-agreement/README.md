# Subject–Verb Agreement

An AI teaching skill for primary-school children, developed for **Second Path**. It turns subject–verb agreement into short explanations, sentence experiments and playful practice.

Designed for Grade 3 learners by default, with Chinese explanations and simple English examples. Language and difficulty can be adjusted.

**[Play the ready-made game](https://hedybi.github.io/second-path-for-kids/skills/subject-verb-agreement/game.html)** — animated lessons, sentence experiments and practice. Or download [game.html](game.html) and open it in a browser.

## What it covers

- Finding the subject and choosing the matching verb.
- Third-person singular forms: **s**, **es**, **ies** and **has**.
- **do / does**, **am / is / are**, and base verbs after **does** or **can**.
- Feedback, retries and practice based on mistakes.

## Start in your AI chat

Open [SKILL.md](SKILL.md), copy its **full contents** into your own AI chat, then paste this prompt:

> Follow the skill above. Teach a Grade 3 child when to use “play” or “plays”. Explain in Chinese with simple English examples. Start now: ask one question at a time and wait for the child's answer. Give a hint after a mistake and let the child try again.

The default is a conversation: explanations, questions, answers and feedback. No coding or installation is needed; results depend on the AI following the instructions.
